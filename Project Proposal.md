# Describe the theme or key idea behind your project
A video game SFX generator inspired by [SFXR](https://sfxr.me/) that leans away from the 8-bit, alien, sci-fi sound palette and into a more natural, bubbly and silly space. Exemplary SFXs are: squeaky rubber, bubble, pop, rubber ducky, etc
The final result will be a synth interface that makes it quick and easy to generate and save quirky SFX.

# Project research and AI collaboration strategy
Discuss examples of current or historic related projects and explain how you plan to strategically collaborate with AI tools during development. How will you maintain creative control while leveraging AI capabilities?

 [SFXR](https://sfxr.me/) is a popular website to extremely quickly generate arcade sounding SFXs for video games, primarily used by indie developers and game jammers. The site has multiple presets for typical SFXs like Jump, Shoot, Coin, etc; and with every click, creates a slightly different variation of the preset, allowing you to for instance explore different sounds for Jumping. In addition to this it has all the slider parameters available to manually tinker with as well as a mutation button and a random button.

 For this project I plan to explain the idea to an LLM, have it generate a [SFXR](https://sfxr.me/) variant to work from

# Project development plan
Explain how your project will work from a user's perspective and outline your approach to balancing independent work with AI collaboration.

## What do you anticipate users to hear?
SFXs in the realms of bubbles, strings, squeaky rubber, pop, rubber ducky, boioings and other silly SFXs.
Maybe some vocal sounding effects if time allows.
## How will users interact with your software?
Since the project is somewhat broad as of now I will start with doing a similar setup as [SFXR](https://sfxr.me/) with rows of sliders that allows you to explore a vast domain of SFXs. As well as a random, mutate and save button. 

## What unique features does your software offer them?
The only uniqueness this software will provide compared to [SFXR](https://sfxr.me/) is a focus on more natural sounding SFX that work well with a silly and colourful aesthetic, possibly moving the indie space slightly away from the worn-out 8-bit aesthetic and into a new space. 

## Planned AI collaboration tools (e.g. ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, etc.)
Primarily ChatGPT as I'm a paying member and therefore can utilize the thinking models. I believe using other models that don't have a thinking component have low success rate; although I will consider exploring others if I'm truly stuck.

## How will you approach AI collaboration during development?
I will use AI as a learning partner as well as a code generator to get me up and running with a starting template, reviewing the code to understand its function in order to manipulate it in the direction I want and to also better explain to the AI where it's falling short or to construct prompts with better specificity.
